# Sol prompt lineage

Sol came from the second prompt pass: build a complete, self-contained procedural Three.js literary interpretation around the supplied passage, narrate that passage exactly, and refine the result through six evidence-backed audiovisual review cycles.

Its distinctive direction is a stylized twilight hill settlement staged like an illustrated story tableau. Fourteen original residents react to the village rumor while a measured 110.8-second master clock drives local narration, phrase captions, camera choreography, character performance, a gold warning motif, and a procedural Web Audio soundscape.

Unlike Luna’s realistic village route, Sol emphasizes compact rounded architecture, theatrical group composition, deterministic seeking, measured phrase timing, and a restrained final transformation from full warning light to a lingering gold echo. The app also carries independent mix controls, orbit and spectator viewing, reduced-motion behavior, and graceful audio/WebGL fallbacks.
